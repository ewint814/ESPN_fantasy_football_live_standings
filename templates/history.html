Each year shows the regular season, the playoff games, then the places next to the sheet. A shaded place is one they do not agree on.

Rules the walk uses
  • The last NFL week does not count. Through 2020 the league ends in week 16. From 2021 it ends in week 17.
  • From 2023, each week is two results: the head-to-head game, plus a win for a top-six score or a loss otherwise.
  • From 2023, the winners' bracket reseeds. The 1 seed plays the lowest seed still alive, and the 2 seed plays the other.
  • From 2025, the 6 seed is the most points outside the top 5. If that team did not play the 3 seed, the walk follows the team that did.
  • In 12-team years, 5th and 6th is the two-week total of the two first-round losers. 9th is the last-week game between the two middle-game teams. The two week-2 bye teams on the losers' side are not a real game.
  • In 10-team years, the winners play two two-week rounds. The losers play one week, one week, then a two-week game, using the single-week scores.
