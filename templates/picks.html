How the odds work
  • The rest of the regular season is played out {{ "{:,}".format(runs) }} times, through week 14.
  • Scores from: ESPN projections uses each roster's best lineup from ESPN's rest-of-season player projections, skipping byes and players on IR (and anyone out this week); this week uses ESPN's own projection. Season average and Last 4 weeks are pulled a little toward the league average, and ESPN's projection counts for half of this week. Blend is half season average, half ESPN.
  • Each week's score varies around that by how much scores have swung this season.
  • Every week has the head-to-head game and the median game: the top half of scores get a win.
  • Seeds 1-5 by record, points for breaking ties. Seed 6 (6th) is the most points among everyone else. Seeds 1-2 get byes.
  • Others pick: Weakest left takes the lowest expected score left. Smart takes whoever gives that team the best playoff odds, this week; later weeks use weakest left. Random takes anyone left, every week.
  • Most chaos tries each pick in turn and keeps the one that leaves the most teams with at least a 1% playoff chance, then the most toss-ups.
  • Odds are rounded and can move by about a point between refreshes.
