How the odds work
  • The rest of the regular season is played out {{ "{:,}".format(runs) }} times, through week 14.
  • Each week a team's score is drawn around what it has averaged this season, pulled a little toward the league average. For the week about to be played, ESPN's projection counts for half.
  • Every week has the head-to-head game and the median game: the top half of scores get a win.
  • Seeds 1-5 by record, points for breaking ties. Seed 6 is the most points among everyone else. Seeds 1-2 get byes.
  • Teams that pick later, this week and in later weeks, are assumed to take the weakest team left.
  • Odds are rounded and can move by about a point between refreshes.
